Privacy Policy

Updated: November 15, 2026

Zone Wonders does not sell collection data or make it available to the developer. The app uses Apple's CloudKit service to sync your collection through your private iCloud database.

Collection Data And iCloud

The app stores artifact checklist state, available quantities, found dates, and optional notes on your device. In sync-enabled versions, when your Apple Account is available, it syncs this content and the history needed to reconcile changes across your devices to your private iCloud database. Version 2.1.1 has no active iCloud sync. Apple provides the iCloud service; the developer has no access to the content in that private database. Its storage counts toward your iCloud quota.

The app keeps an account-scoped local copy and recovery data on your device. Resetting collection progress does not erase notes or all iCloud history. Deleting the app does not necessarily remove data already stored in your iCloud.

Accounts, Analytics, Ads, And Tracking

Zone Wonders uses your system Apple Account for iCloud sync and does not create a separate app account. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. Silent CloudKit notifications can prompt background sync; they do not include your notes or progress in a visible notification.

Device Permissions

The app does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or other protected device resources.

Future Changes

If the app's data practices or third-party services change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those changes are released.

Unofficial Status

Zone Wonders is an unofficial fan-made companion reference app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or approved by GSC Game World.
